FAQ

Quick answers before you order

How does ordering work?

Fill in the order form with what you need. I reply by email or Discord to confirm the details, the price and the timing. Work starts once we've agreed.

What does it cost?

It depends on the job. Pick the budget range closest to what you have in mind and I'll reply with a quote for your specific request.

How long will it take?

That depends on how big the job is and how many orders are ahead of yours. I give you a timeline when I reply to your order.

What should I put in my order?

Describe what you want the feature or build to do. Add a link to your game and any reference videos or screenshots if you have them. The more detail, the more accurate the quote.

What is the order queue?

I only take a limited number of orders at a time so I can do each one properly. The queue counter shows how many spots are open. If it's full, you can still send an order and join the waitlist.

Why isn't my review showing yet?

I check every review before it goes on the page, so there can be a short delay after you send it.

How do I contact you?

Use the order form for work requests, or message me on Discord at .cuban for anything else.

About

I've been messing around in Roblox Studio since I was a kid, and at some point "messing around" turned into actually finishing things: scripting systems that work, building spaces that feel like somewhere, and figuring out the parts nobody tells you about until something breaks.

Most of what I know I picked up by taking something apart in Studio to see why it worked, then trying to build it better myself. I'm still doing that. If you want to talk Lua, level design, or just see what I'm working on, hit me up on Discord.

Want to build something together?
